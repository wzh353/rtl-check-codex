The MES50HP blinker has exactly three external ports, clk, rst_n, and led.
clk is the board's 50 MHz oscillator, rst_n is the active-low KEY1 reset,
and led drives the active-high LED1. Keep the fixed board constraints.
Synchronize release of reset over two rising clock edges. The counter
clears synchronously while reset is active. After reset, increment it by
one on each rising edge. led is the most significant counter bit.
The default COUNTER_BITS is 25. The simulations use smaller widths.
